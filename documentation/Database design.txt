DATABASE DESIGN


1. CUSTOMERS

CustomerID
FirstName
LastName
Email
RegistrationDate

Purpose:
Stores information about each customer.


2. PRODUCTS

ProductID
ProductName
Category
UnitPrice
UnitCost

Purpose:
Stores information about the products sold by the company.

UnitPrice = normal selling price of the product.
UnitCost = cost to the company of acquiring or producing the product.


3. ORDERS

OrderID
CustomerID
OrderDate
ShippingCountry
OrderStatus

Purpose:
Stores information about each customer order.

CustomerID connects each order to the customer who placed it.

OrderStatus can contain values such as:
Completed
Cancelled
Returned


4. ORDER_DETAILS

OrderDetailID
OrderID
ProductID
Quantity
UnitPrice
DiscountPercentage

Purpose:
Stores the individual products contained inside each order.

One order can contain multiple products.
